## The Regime Effect

Performance varies dramatically based on market conditions entering blackout:

| Entry Condition | Count | Avg Excess | Underperf Rate |
|-----------------|-------|------------|----------------|
| **Weak Regime** (below 50MA, >10% off highs, negative momentum) | 4 | **-4.64%** | **75%** |
| Strong Regime (above 50MA, near highs, positive momentum) | 9 | -0.32% | 44% |

**Interpretation:** When Apple enters blackout already weak, the underperformance is 3-4x worse. But this also sets up the late blackout reversal.

## Key Lessons From Apple

1. **The obvious thesis was wrong** - "Short entire blackout" loses money. The edge is in the TIMING.

2. **Front-loaded weakness** - Days 1-14 are where the damage happens. After that, it reverses.

3. **Late blackout is the opportunity** - 77% win rate with +1.79% average excess.

4. **15-day post-earnings is optimal** - Buybacks resume, price recovers, then fades.

5. **Regime matters** - Entering weak amplifies both the early weakness AND the late recovery.

6. **January is special** - Holiday liquidity + year-end dynamics make Q1 earnings unique.
